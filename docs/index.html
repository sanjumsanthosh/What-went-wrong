a post-mortem for habits

You didn't lack discipline.
Something specific went wrong.

Habit apps count streaks; none of them ask why the streak ended. This one runs a short interview — six fixed questions, then follow-ups that probe your actual answers — and returns an analysis in two parts: what went wrong, and what to change next time.

question 3 of 8guided interview

What part of the habit felt most uncomfortable or difficult for you?

Consider the moments when you felt resistance — "waking up early to run was the hardest part for me."

how it works

Fixed opening, adaptive middle, blunt ending

The script is deliberately boring at the start — habit, substitute behaviour, friction, timing, obstacles — so the follow-ups have something real to work with.

01

Six fixed questions

Same ground every session: what the habit was, what replaced it, where the resistance lived, when you did it, what got in the way.

02

Adaptive follow-ups

Two questions written from your answers — not from a template. They target the pattern you described.

03

Two-section analysis

JSON with whatWentWrong and whatToDoNext, streamed as it's generated.

the output

Pattern first, then a change small enough to keep

What went wrong

  • The habit was scheduled at your lowest-energy hour, then judged as a willpower failure.
  • The substitute behaviour (scrolling) was the default because nothing competed with it.
  • Missing one day became missing a week — no restart rule existed.

What to do next

  • Move the habit to the slot where it already competes with nothing.
  • Define the two-minute version, so a bad day still counts.
  • Write the restart rule before you need it: "miss once, resume tomorrow, no catch-up."
